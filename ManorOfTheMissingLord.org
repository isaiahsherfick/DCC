    The setting in which this adventure takes place is an ordinary instance
of feudal society. Peasants toil about their daily lives, farming simple crops,
raising typical livestock, and producing basic textiles. Over these peasants
presides a lord. This lord is an arrogant prick who produces nothing useful. He
frequently visits his peasantry and demands arbitrary taxes and dues with little
rhyme or reason. He is not popular among the peasantry, but this is the way of
things. It is easiest to simply grumble "yes, milord", and produce whatever he
demands. Sometimes it is coin. Sometimes it is the ripest crops of the harvest
or the choicest cuts of meat. Sometimes it is the eldest son for a territory
dispute with a neighboring lord. Again, such is the way of things.  Refusing to
comply is a death sentence at best.
   This has been the way of things for as long as anyone nearby can remember. At
least, it was before this latest harvest. As the leaves of autumn settle beneath the chilling winds of change that foreshadow another harsh winter, it marks the hundredth day of complete silence from the lord -- completely unheard of.
